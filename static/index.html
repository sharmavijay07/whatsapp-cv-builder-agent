The conversation

Six steps. Generation waits for you.

The agent asks for one thing at a time. A duplicate WhatsApp webhook does not start a second pack.

01

Name

Safu introduces itself and asks for the name that should sit at the top of the CV.

02

Your CV

PDF, Word, a photo (OCR), a link, or pasted text. The file is read into a structured record.

03

The job

Paste the post, send a screenshot, or share a URL. Safu pulls the title, company, and keywords.

04

Confirm

You see the employers, dates, and education it extracted. Reply YES, or correct a contact field.

05

Payment

The pack price is shown. M-Pesa, a Paystack test checkout, or the demo stub. Documents wait until payment is confirmed.

06

The pack

Word and PDF files, scores, a cover letter, and interview notes arrive in the chat.

What gets checked

Invented facts are rejected.

The draft is compared with the record extracted from your CV. Employers, dates, qualifications, and figures have to already be in that record.

Employers

A company that was not on the CV cannot appear on the rewrite.

Dates

A role cannot quietly move to an earlier year.

Qualifications

A diploma stays a diploma. A degree is not added.

Figures

Team sizes, percentages, and counts have to come from the source text.

Data

The database stores the order, not the CV.

Stored

Order id, step, paid flag, hand-off flag, delivery attempts, and the WhatsApp message id used to ignore retries.

Deleted

Generated files expire 30 minutes after delivery. The working copy of the CV is removed once the pack is in your hands. An unfinished order gets one follow-up after 48 hours.

If a step fails, Safu retries from the files it already built or from the short-lived working copy. After repeated failure it flags a person, and it tells you to send the CV again rather than inventing a document.

Under the hood

Groq by default. Other models are a setting.

Groq

Default model openai/gpt-oss-120b on the free tier, through the OpenAI-compatible API. Gemini, OpenAI, and Claude stay available via config.

WhatsApp

Meta Cloud API when you go live. This demo uses a fake chat so the full order runs with no token.

Order state

Memory in the demo. Supabase free tier when you want the rows to survive a restart. No CV column exists in the schema.

Price

KES 1,500 for one application.

That is the price shown inside the chat. The demo stub records PAY and charges nothing. Live mode can send an M-Pesa prompt or a Paystack test link, and the documents go out only after that payment is confirmed.

What if the model is unsure?

Say HUMAN, or Safu will hand off after a CV it cannot read. A person is alerted, and the bot stops writing.

Can I edit the CV?

Yes. The Word file is a single column with normal headings. The PDF matches it for sending onward.

What does the sample candidate represent?

Amina Wanjiku and Freightline Kenya are fictional, so the demo can run without anyone's real CV.
